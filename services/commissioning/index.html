Service 03

Flushing, pressure testing and commissioning

Before a single rack is connected, the loop is pressure-tested, cleaned, flushed, disinfected, filled and analysed, against pass or fail acceptance criteria.

Why commissioning decides the life of the hardware.

In direct liquid cooling, the coolant flows through cold plates with very fine channels, mounted directly on the processors. Particles left by the installation, the wrong chemistry or bacterial growth can clog or corrode them.

Server manufacturers therefore set requirements on the cleanliness and chemistry of the coolant. A loop that does not meet them puts the hardware, and its warranty, at risk. Commissioning is the stage where this is proven, in writing, before the racks are connected.

Acceptance criteria, pass or fail

  • ParticlesThe 5 µm filter is clean at the end of the cycle.
  • ChemistrypH, conductivity, glycol and inhibitor within the manufacturer's thresholds.
  • MicrobiologyBacterial count below the manufacturer's threshold.
  • GasHigh points vented, loop degassed.

Our protocol

Flushing, disinfection, filling: 6 steps.

The loop is first proven leak-tight by a recorded hydraulic pressure test. Then:

Preparation

Racks and CDUs are isolated. Flushing connections and a mobile skid are installed.

Cleaning

Chemical degreasing, following your procedure or ours.

Flushing

Section by section, in stages from 100 to 50 to 5 µm, above the nominal flow rate.

Disinfection

Biocide with a controlled contact time, then a final rinse.

Filling

Premixed coolant such as PG25, filled from the low point, then venting and degassing.

Analysis

Laboratory analysis before the racks are connected, and a signed report.

Commissioning levels

From L1 to L5.

Data center commissioning is usually organised in 5 levels. We take the loop through all of them, from the checks on the equipment before it ships to the integrated test of the loop with the rest of the site.

L1Factory tests of the equipment, before it ships.

L3Start-up of each piece of equipment.

Questions about commissioning.

How is a liquid cooling loop flushed before start-up?

The racks and CDUs are isolated, the loop is chemically degreased, then flushed section by section through progressively finer filters, from 100 to 50 to 5 µm, at a flow above the nominal rate. It is then disinfected with a biocide, rinsed, filled with premixed coolant from the low point, vented and degassed. A laboratory analysis is done before the racks are connected.

Why is flushing necessary on a new loop?

A newly installed loop contains particles, grease and residues from the installation. In direct liquid cooling, the coolant passes through cold plates with very fine channels: particles can clog them, and poor chemistry can corrode them. Flushing, disinfection and analysis prove that the fluid meets the hardware manufacturer's requirements before the racks are connected.

What is in the handover dossier?

Joint-by-joint traceability, the reports of the pressure test and of the acceptance tests, a dated water analysis, the as-built drawings and the operating procedures.
