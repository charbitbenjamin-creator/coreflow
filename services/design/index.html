Liquid cooling design and design review

We design the loop between the facility water and the racks in 3D BIM, or we review the design you already have, before anything is built.

What the design covers.

A liquid cooling loop is decided on paper long before the first pipe is installed: the choice of CDUs, the route through the room, the diameters, the position of every sensor. We come in as early as possible, because that is where delays and rework are avoided.

  • CDU selection and redundancyType, capacity and redundancy scheme, in-row or at the demarcation point, and their integration in the room.
  • RoutingThe path of the pipework in the room, under a raised floor or overhead, around existing equipment.
  • Hydraulic sizingHydraulic calculations and balancing, pipe diameters, expansion vessels.
  • InstrumentationTemperature, pressure, flow and leak detection: what is measured, where, and how it is reported.
  • Drawings and modelP&ID, 3D BIM model and isometric drawings.

2 ways to use our design team.

Full design

You have a hardware order and a room, but no liquid design yet. We design the loop in 3D BIM, from the CDUs to the connection of each rack, and we can then deliver it end to end.

Design review

Your design exists, drawn by your engineering office or by a manufacturer. We check sizing, routing and instrumentation, and whether it can be built and commissioned as drawn, before work starts.

Planned backwards from the hardware delivery date.

The racks arrive on a date that does not move. Design is often on the critical path of a liquid-cooled deployment, so we start from that date and work backwards: design, procurement, installation, commissioning.

The 3D model and the calculation notes are validated before work begins. The result is fewer surprises and less rework on site.

What we need to start

  • Number of racks and power per rack, in kW
  • Temperatures and delta T, from the facility and from the hardware manufacturer
  • CDU configuration and type of redundancy
  • Room drawings and distances
  • Raised floor or overhead distribution
  • Specified fluid and manufacturer requirements

Questions about design.

Can CoreFlow work from a design made by someone else?

Yes. When the design is already fixed, we review it, then step in on execution alone: installation, commissioning and handover. The review checks sizing, routing and instrumentation before work starts.

What does a liquid cooling design deliver?

A P&ID, hydraulic calculations and balancing, the routing of the pipework, a 3D BIM model with isometric drawings, the selection and redundancy of the CDUs, and the instrumentation plan for temperature, pressure, flow and leak detection.

Why design in 3D BIM?

A 3D BIM model places the loop in the real room, with the racks, the raised floor or the ceiling and the existing equipment. Clashes are found on screen and not on site, and the same model is updated into the as-built drawings handed over at the end of the project.
