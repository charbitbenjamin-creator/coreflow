Service 04

Monitoring and maintenance of liquid cooling loops

After go-live, we monitor temperature, pressure, flow and leaks, and we maintain the loop for the life of the system: preventive maintenance, water analysis and on-call support.

A loop is not finished on the day it starts.

A closed loop changes over time. The corrosion inhibitor is consumed, warm water favours bacterial growth, filters load up, air collects at high points. None of this is visible from the data hall until a temperature rises or a flow drops.

Monitoring and maintenance keep the loop in the state it had at handover, and keep the proof of it.

What we monitor

  • Temperature, pressure and flowReported to your BMS.
  • LeaksZoned leak detection, with isolation rack by rack.
  • Fluid chemistryFollowed over time against your thresholds and the manufacturer's.

Questions about maintenance.

What does liquid cooling maintenance include?

3 building blocks that can be combined: preventive maintenance of the loop (pressure drop across filters, air venting, joints and supports, sensor calibration), water analysis (pH, conductivity, glycol and inhibitor concentration, bacterial count) and on-call support with a response time fixed in the contract.

Why does the coolant need to be analysed over time?

In a closed loop, the corrosion inhibitor is consumed and warm water favours bacterial growth. Regular analysis of pH, conductivity, glycol and inhibitor concentration and bacterial count shows drift early, before it damages the cold plates or reduces the flow.

What happens if there is a leak?

The on-call team intervenes within the response time fixed in the contract: the affected section is locked out, repaired, then refilled, and the refill is traced. A stock of spare parts is sized with the client in advance.
