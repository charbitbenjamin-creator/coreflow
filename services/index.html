Services

Liquid cooling services, from design to maintenance

CoreFlow covers the whole chain of a data center liquid cooling loop: design, supply and installation, commissioning and handover, monitoring and maintenance. Entrust us with all of it, or with a single stage.

4 services, 1 accountable team.

The stages follow each other without a handoff between companies: the people who design the loop are the ones who commission it and maintain it.

Design and design review

3D BIM model, CDU selection and redundancy, routing, sizing and instrumentation. Or a review of the design you already have.

Design in detail

Supply and installation

CDUs, stainless pipework, insulation, pre-assembled manifolds and sensors, including in data halls that stay in operation.

Installation in detail

Commissioning and handover

Pressure testing, flushing, disinfection, filling and analysis, then a full quality dossier: traceability, test reports, as-built drawings.

Commissioning in detail

Monitoring and maintenance

Monitoring of temperature, pressure, flow and leaks. Preventive maintenance, water analysis and on-call support.

Maintenance in detail

Scope

What is in our scope, element by element.

Our scope is the full loop between the facility water and the racks, CDUs included.

ElementWhat we doStatus

Who we work for

3 types of clients.

AI clouds

GPU clusters to bring online on a fixed date, in your own data center or in colocation. We plan backwards from the hardware delivery date.

Enterprise data centers

A first liquid-cooled room, an extension or an air-to-liquid conversion, on a site that stays in operation.

Cooling manufacturers

A local second line for the maintenance of your installed equipment: preventive maintenance, water analysis and on-call support.
