Service 02

CDU and pipework supply and installation

We supply and install the CDUs, the stainless pipework, the insulation, the pre-assembled manifolds and the sensors, including in data halls that stay in operation.

What we supply and install.

Everything between the facility water and the racks, as one package and under one responsibility.

CDUs

Supplied and installed, in-row or at the demarcation point. We are independent of cooling manufacturers and select the units for your project.

Stainless pipework

316L stainless steel, press-fit or welded depending on the site, with supports and valves.

Insulation

Insulation of the pipework, to your specifications.

Pre-assembled manifolds

Manifolds prepared outside the data hall and delivered ready to install, to reduce the time spent in the room.

Sensors and leak detection

Temperature, pressure, flow and leak detection, installed with the pipework.

Rack connection

The connection at each rack, ready for the hardware when it arrives.

Live sites

Working in a data hall that stays in operation.

Many liquid cooling projects are extensions or air-to-liquid conversions in a room where servers are already running. The method matters as much as the pipework.

  • A method of procedure for each interventionEach MOP is integrated in your change management process.
  • A joining method suited to the sitePress-fit in sensitive areas, qualified welding outside the data hall.
  • Leak risk under controlDrip trays, zoned leak detection and isolation rack by rack. The emergency procedure for a leak is tested before the loop is filled.
  • Qualified peopleStainless steel pipefitters and welders. Welder qualifications to ISO 9606-1 and welding procedure qualifications are provided before work starts.

Every joint traced.

Installation produces evidence as it goes. Each joint is identified and recorded, so that the conformity of the loop to the approved design can be checked joint by joint.

These records feed the quality dossier you receive at handover, together with the test reports and the as-built drawings.

What you receive at handover

What we need to plan the work

  • Room drawings and distances
  • Raised floor or overhead distribution
  • Access rules, MOP and change management process
  • Hardware delivery date
  • Design of the loop, if it already exists

Questions about installation.

Does CoreFlow supply the CDUs?

Yes. CDUs are part of our scope: we select them with you, supply them and install them, in-row or at the demarcation point. CoreFlow is a service team, independent of cooling manufacturers.

Can the work be done while the data center is running?

Yes. Extensions and air-to-liquid conversions are done on sites that stay in operation, with a method of procedure for each intervention, integrated in the operator's change management, and with leak risk controlled by drip trays, zoned detection and isolation rack by rack.

Which materials and joining methods does CoreFlow use?

316L stainless steel pipework, press-fit or welded depending on the site. Press-fit is preferred in sensitive areas, and qualified welding is done outside the data hall. Manifolds are pre-assembled before they enter the room.

An installation to plan?

Tell us about the room, the racks and the date. We come back with a scope, a schedule and a price.
