About

A liquid cooling service team, independent of manufacturers

CoreFlow was founded in 2026 to do one thing: deliver the liquid cooling loop of European data centers from design to maintenance, with a single partner accountable end to end.

Why CoreFlow exists.

The latest generations of AI racks draw more than 100 kW each and can no longer be cooled with air. Liquid cooling has become a hardware requirement, and every new GPU cluster comes with a loop of pipework, CDUs and manifolds that has to be designed, built, cleaned and kept in specification for years.

This loop sits between several trades. The facility delivers its water, the hardware manufacturer sets its requirements, and the work in between is split across companies. Yet a leak or a fluid-quality issue means downtime and damaged GPUs, on racks worth several million dollars each.

CoreFlow is a service company built for that loop. We come in as early as possible, with the design or its review. We supply and install the CDUs, the pipework, the insulation, the pre-assembled manifolds and the sensors. We flush, disinfect, pressure-test and commission the loop. Then we monitor and maintain it.

Our first projects are in preparation.

How we work

3 principles.

One accountable team

Not a chain of subcontractors. Design, installation, commissioning and maintenance answer to the same people, from the first drawing to years of operation.

The same written procedures

From one site to the next, across Europe: the same methods, the same acceptance criteria, the same handover dossier.

Evidence, not statements

A recorded pressure test, joint-by-joint traceability, a dated water analysis. What we commit to can be checked.

Our know-how

Hyperscale cooling experience, applied to your loop.

Our team comes from the cooling teams of a European hyperscaler, where it designed, built, converted and maintained cooling systems at scale.

Over 50 cooling projects

New builds, retrofits and air-to-liquid conversions, including 3 direct liquid cooling deployments in 2025 and 2026.

Water quality since 2012

Water treatment engineering, the discipline that keeps a liquid loop clean and in specification for years.

Standards we wrote

The site maintenance and water quality standards of a European hyperscaler were written by our team.
