What we need from you.

You do not need everything to start the conversation. This is the list we work through together.

Power and temperatures

  • Number of racks, kW per rack
  • Temperatures and delta T, from the facility and the hardware manufacturer
  • CDU configuration, type of redundancy

Layout

  • Room drawings and distances
  • Raised floor or overhead distribution
  • Access, MOP and change management

Fluid and operation

  • Specified fluid, manufacturer requirements
  • Your flushing procedure, if you have one
  • BMS, DCIM and quality tools
