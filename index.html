Who we are

A service team, not a product vendor.

CoreFlow is independent of cooling manufacturers. We work on your design or on one we build with you.

A leak or a fluid-quality issue means downtime and damaged GPUs, on racks worth several million dollars each. That is why a single team stays accountable from the first drawing to years of operation: we design or review the loop, supply and install it, flush, pressure-test and commission it, then monitor and maintain it.

  • Independent of cooling manufacturersWe select CDUs and components for your project, not for a catalogue.
  • The same written procedures from one site to the nextAcross Europe, every project follows the same methods and acceptance criteria.
  • One accountable team, not a chain of subcontractorsDesign, installation, commissioning and maintenance answer to the same people.

What we deliver

The whole chain, in 5 stages.

Entrust us with the full loop or with a single stage. Each stage ends with documents you can check.

Design

3D BIM model, CDU selection and redundancy, routing, sizing, instrumentation.

Supply and install

CDUs, stainless pipework, insulation, pre-assembled manifolds and sensors.

Commission

Flushing, disinfection, pressure testing, commissioning of the loop.

Hand over

Joint-by-joint traceability, test reports, as-built drawings.

Monitor, maintain

Monitoring of temperature, pressure, flow and leaks. Preventive maintenance, water analysis, on-call.

CoreFlow delivers liquid cooling for European data centers from design to maintenance. We design or review the loop in 3D BIM, supply and install the CDUs, pipework, insulation, pre-assembled manifolds and sensors, flush, pressure-test and commission the loop, then monitor and maintain it for the life of the system.
